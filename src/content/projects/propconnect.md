Built for the Sinulog PropTech Hackathon 2026 (*Code to Keys: AI for Real Estate*), where our team reached the grand finals. PropConnect connects tenants with landlords and uses AI to do the legwork:

- **AI property discovery:** recommendations based on preferences, budget and location.
- **Map view:** explore listings on an interactive Mapbox map with street, satellite and 3D views.
- **Real-time chat:** tenants and landlords message each other instantly (Firebase).
- **Dashboards:** landlords manage listings and photos, and tenants keep favourites, recently viewed homes and active rentals.
- **Accounts:** separate tenant and landlord sign-up with verification and email notifications.
