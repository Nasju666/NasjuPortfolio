Small store owners often record sales by hand, don't know their real profit, and can't prove their income when they apply for a loan. Kita fixes that from the phone they already use:

- **Point of sale:** record sales at the counter, with totals, change and stock deducted automatically.
- **Receipt scanning:** photograph a supplier receipt and the AI reads the items, logs the expense and updates inventory.
- **Inventory:** real-time stock levels with low-stock alerts.
- **Dashboard and AI insights:** sales, expenses and profit trends, with plain-language advice when sales drop or costs rise.
- **Loan Readiness Report:** a shareable summary of sales, expenses, profit and stability that works as alternative credit data for microfinance lenders like RAFI.
- **KYC verification:** ID, selfie and store photo checks, so lenders can trust that the business is real.
