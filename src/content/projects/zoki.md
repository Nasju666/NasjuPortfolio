A wedding planning marketplace for Cebu: engaged couples find and book local vendors, from photographers and caterers to venues and entertainment, and plan the whole wedding within their budget. I led development on a small team of developers.

- **Three roles in one app:** customers, vendors, and an admin panel for users, vendors, bookings, subscriptions and reports.
- **For couples:** browse vendors by category and location, vendor pages with showcase videos, bookings, a budget tracker, a wedding checklist, favorites and vouchers.
- **For vendors:** service listings, booking inquiries, a calendar, vouchers, and a subscription through Google Play Billing.
- **Real-time chat** between couples and vendors, built on Supabase Realtime, plus push notifications with per-user preferences.
- **Philippine addresses** down to the barangay, using official PSGC location data.
- **Works offline:** recent data is cached, and changes made offline are queued and sent once the phone is back online.
